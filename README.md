# Business Learn Studio (`business_learn_studio`)

**NESA Stage 6 HSC Year 12 Business Studies — Interactive Syllabus Note-Taking, Multimedia Masterclass & Word/PDF Export Platform**

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Complete Official NESA Year 12 HSC Syllabus Coverage (RIPS / RIPSE)**
   - **⚙️ Operations (25%)**: Role, Influences, Processes (IPO Model, 4Vs, Gantt/CPA), Strategies (6 Performance Objectives, SCM, Outsourcing, Tech, Inventory LIFO/FIFO/JIT, Quality QC/QA/TQM, Change & Global).
   - **📣 Marketing (25%)**: Role, Influences (PEGS, ACL Consumer Laws, Ethics/Sugging), Processes (6-Step Framework: SWOT/PLC, Market Research, SMART Objectives, Target Markets, 4Ps, Monitoring/Control), Strategies (Segmentation, Differentiation/Positioning, Extended 7Ps, E-Marketing, Global Marketing / Glocalisation).
   - **📊 Finance (25%)**: Role (PLEGS Objectives & Short vs Long-term conflicts), Influences (Internal vs External Debt/Equity, Institutions, ASIC/Tax, Global), Processes (Planning Cycle, Matching Principle, 3 Financial Statements, 7 Ratios + Interactive Ratio Simulator, 6 Limitations & Ethics), Strategies (Cash Flow, Working Capital Leasing/Sale & Lease-Back, Profitability Cost/Revenue Controls, Global Payment Methods, Hedging & Derivatives).
   - **👥 Human Resources (25%)**: Role, Influences (6 Stakeholders, Fair Work Act 2009, 11 NES, Modern Awards, Enterprise Agreements/BOOT, Economic/Tech/Social/Ethics), Processes (Acquisition, Development, Maintenance, Separation), Strategies (Leadership, Job Design, Recruitment, Training, Performance Management, Rewards, Global Staffing Ethnocentric/Polycentric/Geocentric, Workplace Disputes), and **Effectiveness Indicators (RIPSE)**.

2. **Structured 2-Part Dot-Point Cards + Mastery Tracking**
   - **Part 1 — Core Theory & Definitions**: Guided NESA syllabus prompts, sub-point breakdowns, live word count, and click-to-insert **Key Syllabus Terms**.
   - **Part 2 — Case Study Application & Band 6 Evidence**: Dedicated case study workspace with one-click **McDonald's / Apple / Qantas** exemplar facts and statistics.
   - **Mastery Status Toggle**: Mark each dot point as `⚪ Not Started`, `🟡 Reviewing`, or `🟢 Mastered`, and filter the syllabus by status or keyword.

3. **Integrated Gemini NotebookLM Videos, Infographics & PDF Study Decks**
   - Direct launch button to the companion **Gemini NotebookLM** (`https://notebook.google.com/notebook/96479e04-bdd6-457b-9cea-a1b3624e895e`).
   - Built-in HTML5 Video Player for 9 syllabus & Band 6 exam masterclasses (`assets/videos/`).
   - High-resolution lightbox viewer for 8 visual syllabus blueprints (`assets/infographics/`).
   - Embedded PDF study guides including *Operating Under Pressure (McDonald's Operations)* and *The Golden Arches of Finance* (`assets/docs/`).

4. **One-Click Microsoft Word (`.doc`), Print/PDF & JSON Exports**
   - **Download Word (`.doc`) — Current Topic or Full Syllabus**: Generates a landscape Microsoft Word table containing every syllabus dot point, the student's Core Theory notes, Case Study Evidence, Mastery status, and Financial Ratios.
   - **Download Blank Word Worksheet (`.doc`)**: Exports a clean worksheet template for offline typing or printing.
   - **Print / Save as PDF**: Opens a print-styled study booklet ready for `Save as PDF`.
   - **Auto-Save + JSON Backup + Teacher Dashboard (`teacher.html`)**.
