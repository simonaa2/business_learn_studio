5 Economic Effects of Quotas vs. Tariff Quotas vs. Tariffs

Economic Effect Import Quota (PW → PQ) Tariff Quota Import Tariff (PW → PT)
1. Protection (Supply) Effect Domestic supply expands along S from 0Q1 to 0Q3. Domestic supply expands as above-quota tariff raises domestic price. Domestic supply expands along S from 0Q1 to 0Q3.
2. Consumption (Demand) Effect Domestic demand contracts along D from 0Q2 to 0Q4 due to higher price PQ. Domestic demand contracts as consumers face higher retail prices. Domestic demand contracts along D from 0Q2 to 0Q4 due to higher price PT.
3. Import Volume Effect Guaranteed cap on import volume (fixed at Q3Q4); price adjusts to clear market. Imports up to quota pay standard rate; imports above quota pay higher punitive tariff. Import volume falls indirectly (Q1Q2 → Q3Q4) depending on price elasticity of D & S.
4. Redistribution Effect Consumer surplus is transferred to domestic producers (green trapezium). Consumer surplus is transferred to domestic producers and government. Consumer surplus is transferred to domestic producers (green trapezium).
5. Revenue Effect No government revenue (goes to import licence holders as Quota Rent unless auctioned). Generates government tariff revenue on imports (combining quota cap + revenue). Government collects Tariff Revenue equal to box ABCD (Tariff × Q3Q4).
